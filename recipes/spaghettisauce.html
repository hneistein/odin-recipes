Spaghetti Sauce

Spaghetti noodles in a tomato meat sauce.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds ground Italian sausage or beef
  • 3 medium onions, chopped
  • 2 cups sliced mushrooms
  • 6 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 2 cans (14 1/2 ounces each) diced tomatoes, undrained
  • 1 can (29 ounces) tomato sauce
  • 1 can (12 ounces) tomato paste
  • 3 tablespoons Italian spices
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper

Steps

  1. Cook sausage / beef, onions, mushrooms, and garlic in 12-inch skillet over medium heat about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until sausage is no longer pink; drain.
  2. Spoon sausage mixture into 5-quart slow cooker. Stir in remaining ingredients.
  3. Cover and cook on low heat setting 8 to 9 hours or until vegetables are tender.
